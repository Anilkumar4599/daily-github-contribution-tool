

\# Inventory Reconciliation Checklist



\*\*Project:\*\* Day 143 - Inventory Reconciliation

\*\*Report Date:\*\* 23-Nov-2026

\*\*Domain:\*\* Inventory Management



\## 1. Objective



To verify that physical inventory quantities match the inventory system records and identify stock differences.



\## 2. Pre-Reconciliation Checklist



\- \[ ] Confirm the inventory reconciliation date.

\- \[ ] Obtain the latest inventory system stock report.

\- \[ ] Obtain the latest stock-in transaction report.

\- \[ ] Obtain the latest stock-out transaction report.

\- \[ ] Confirm all receipts and issues are recorded.

\- \[ ] Identify pending purchase receipts.

\- \[ ] Identify pending material issues.

\- \[ ] Freeze or record stock movements during physical counting.

\- \[ ] Confirm item codes and descriptions.

\- \[ ] Assign responsibility for physical stock counting.



\## 3. Stock Calculation Checklist



\- \[ ] Verify opening stock quantity.

\- \[ ] Verify total stock-in quantity.

\- \[ ] Verify total stock-out quantity.

\- \[ ] Calculate expected closing stock.

\- \[ ] Compare calculated closing stock with recorded system stock.

\- \[ ] Record physical stock quantity.

\- \[ ] Calculate physical stock variance.



\### Calculation Formulas



Expected Closing Stock = Opening Stock + Stock-In - Stock-Out



Physical Variance = Physical Stock - System Stock



\## 4. Physical Verification Checklist



\- \[ ] Physically count all selected inventory items.

\- \[ ] Verify item code and item description.

\- \[ ] Check material condition.

\- \[ ] Identify damaged or unusable stock.

\- \[ ] Check for duplicate or incorrect item identification.

\- \[ ] Verify material stored in different locations.

\- \[ ] Record physical count results.



\## 5. Variance Investigation Checklist



\- \[ ] Investigate physical stock shortages.

\- \[ ] Investigate physical stock excess.

\- \[ ] Check missing stock-in entries.

\- \[ ] Check missing stock-out entries.

\- \[ ] Check duplicate transactions.

\- \[ ] Check incorrect item codes.

\- \[ ] Check pending goods receipts or material issues.

\- \[ ] Document the root cause of each variance.

\- \[ ] Obtain approval before inventory adjustment.



\## 6. Reconciliation Closure Checklist



\- \[ ] Complete the reconciliation summary.

\- \[ ] Record all identified variances.

\- \[ ] Document corrective actions.

\- \[ ] Obtain approval from the responsible manager.

\- \[ ] Update inventory records after approval.

\- \[ ] Verify adjusted balances.

\- \[ ] Save reconciliation evidence and supporting documents.

\- \[ ] Close the reconciliation activity.



\## 7. Test Cases



| Test ID | Scenario | Expected Result |

|---|---|---|

| TC-001 | Physical stock equals system stock | Matched |

| TC-002 | Physical stock is lower than system stock | Physical Shortage |

| TC-003 | Physical stock is higher than system stock | Physical Excess |

| TC-004 | Calculated closing differs from recorded system stock | System Balance Mismatch |



\## 8. Reconciliation Responsibilities



| Activity | Responsible Team |

|---|---|

| System stock report | Inventory Team |

| Physical stock count | Warehouse / Inventory Team |

| Transaction verification | Procurement / Inventory Team |

| Variance investigation | Inventory / Procurement |

| Corrective action approval | Authorized Manager |

| Final reconciliation | Inventory Manager |



\## 9. Important Assumptions



1\. Sample records are illustrative and not actual company inventory.

2\. All quantities are measured in units.

3\. Opening stock is the balance at the beginning of the reconciliation period.

4\. All stock movements should be recorded before reconciliation.

5\. Physical stock is counted on the reconciliation date.

6\. Inventory adjustments require proper investigation and approval.

7\. Damaged and unusable stock must be separately identified.
