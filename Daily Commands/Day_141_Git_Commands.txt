## 9. Assumptions

1. Report date is fixed at 21-Nov-2026.
2. Inventory age is calculated using calendar days.
3. Each CSV row represents one inventory stock lot.
4. Received date is used as the starting point for aging.
5. Stock value = Quantity on Hand × Unit Cost.
6. Sample records are illustrative and not actual company inventory.
7. Stock movements, returns, reservations, and subsequent issues are not considered.
