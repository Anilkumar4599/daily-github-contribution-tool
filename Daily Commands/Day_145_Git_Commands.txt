## 4. Calculation Logic

- Current Stock less than or equal to Minimum Stock: REORDER NOW.
- Current Stock greater than Minimum and less than or equal to Maximum: STOCK OK.
- Current Stock greater than Maximum: ABOVE MAXIMUM.

Reorder Quantity = Maximum Stock − Current Stock when replenishment is required.
