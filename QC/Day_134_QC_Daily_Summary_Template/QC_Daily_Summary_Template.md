\# Part D - Data Validation Checklist



Before releasing a daily QC summary, verify:



1\. Kits Received = Kits Inspected + Pending Inspection.

2\. Kits Inspected = Passed on First Inspection + Failed on First Inspection.

3\. Initially Failed = Passed After Rework + Pending Rework,

&#x20;  where every failed kit is accounted for in the rework process.

4\. Passed After Rework must not exceed Rework Completed.

5\. Defective counts must not be negative.

6\. Inspection Coverage, FPY and Failure Rate must be

&#x20;  calculated using the correct denominator.

7\. All open issues must have an owner and next action.



\## Business Value



A standardised daily QC summary helps management:



\- Monitor daily QC performance.

\- Identify critical defect categories.

\- Track rework and pending inspections.

\- Assign responsibility for corrective actions.

\- Improve supplier and device quality follow-up.
