\# QC Stock In vs Stock Out Analysis



\## Project Overview



This project demonstrates a simple inventory stock-in versus stock-out

analysis using hypothetical QC inventory data.



\## Objective



The objective is to understand inventory movement by comparing:



\- Opening stock

\- Stock received

\- Stock issued

\- Closing stock



\## Formula



Closing Stock = Opening Stock + Stock In - Stock Out



\## Validation



The project includes validation for:



\- Blank item names

\- Numeric quantities

\- Negative quantities

\- Stock-out exceeding available stock

\- Closing-stock reconciliation



\## Sample Result



Total Opening Stock = 360



Total Stock In = 170



Total Stock Out = 260



Total Closing Stock = 270



Validation:



360 + 170 - 260 = 270



Result: PASS



\## Business Use



This type of analysis can help QC and inventory management teams monitor:



\- Fast-moving items

\- Stock shortages

\- Procurement requirements

\- Inventory consumption

\- Shipment requirements



\## Disclaimer



This is a hypothetical learning project and does not represent actual

company inventory data.
