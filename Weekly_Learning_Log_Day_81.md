1\. All ATM IDs and records in this dataset are fictional.

2\. The dataset is created only for learning and demonstration.

3\. The status values are assumed to reflect the monitoring state on the

&#x20;  listed date.

4\. Alerts are simple counts; their severity and causes are not provided.

5\. An offline status does not, by itself, establish the reason for an outage.

6\. No real AWS services or ATM devices are connected to this dataset.

7\. The sample is too small to draw conclusions about an actual fleet.

8\. No cost, availability, or performance claims are made about a deployed

&#x20;  cloud solution.
