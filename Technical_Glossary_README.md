\## Input Validation



The glossary includes a sample validation exercise that checks:



1\. Required fields are not blank.

2\. Categories belong to an approved list.

3\. Duplicate terms are flagged for review.



The sample records are fictional and are used only to demonstrate validation logic.



\## Assumptions



\- Definitions are simplified for beginner-level learning.

\- The validation exercise is documented pseudocode, not an implemented program.

\- Technical terms may have additional meanings depending on the organization or project.
