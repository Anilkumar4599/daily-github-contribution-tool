\# QC KPI Definitions — ATM Surveillance Devices



\## 1. Purpose



This document defines important Quality Control KPIs used to monitor

inspection performance, product quality, defects, rework and shipment

readiness.



This is a hypothetical learning example.



\---



\## 2. KPI Summary



| KPI | Definition | Formula | Example Result |

|---|---|---|---:|

| FPY | Percentage of units passing QC first time | First-Time Pass / Total Inspected × 100 | 92% |

| Defect Rate | Percentage of inspected units with defects | Defective Units / Total Inspected × 100 | 8% |

| Rework Rate | Percentage of units requiring rework | Reworked Units / Total Inspected × 100 | 6% |

| Critical Defect Rate | Percentage of critical defects | Critical Defects / Total Defects × 100 | 25% |

| Shipment Pass Rate | Percentage of units cleared for shipment | Units Passed / Units Inspected × 100 | 94% |

| First-Time Rejection Rate | Percentage rejected during first inspection | First-Time Rejections / Total Inspected × 100 | 8% |



\---



\## 3. KPI 1 — First-Pass Yield (FPY)



\### Definition



FPY measures the percentage of units that pass QC during the first

inspection without rework, repair or correction.



\### Formula



FPY (%) = First-Time Pass Units / Total Units Inspected × 100



\### Example Input



\- Total Units Inspected = 100

\- First-Time Pass Units = 92



\### Calculation



92 / 100 × 100 = 92%



\### Expected Output



\*\*FPY = 92%\*\*



\### Management Interpretation



A 92% FPY means that 92 out of 100 inspected units passed QC during

the first inspection without requiring rework.



\---



\## 4. KPI 2 — Defect Rate



\### Definition



Defect Rate measures the percentage of inspected units that contain

one or more defects.



\### Formula



Defect Rate (%) = Defective Units / Total Units Inspected × 100



\### Example Input



\- Total Units Inspected = 100

\- Defective Units = 8



\### Calculation



8 / 100 × 100 = 8%



\### Expected Output



\*\*Defect Rate = 8%\*\*



\---



\## 5. KPI 3 — Rework Rate



\### Definition



Rework Rate measures the percentage of inspected units that require

rework before they can be released.



\### Formula



Rework Rate (%) = Reworked Units / Total Units Inspected × 100



\### Example Input



\- Total Units Inspected = 100

\- Reworked Units = 6



\### Calculation



6 / 100 × 100 = 6%



\### Expected Output



\*\*Rework Rate = 6%\*\*



\---



\## 6. KPI 4 — Critical Defect Rate



\### Definition



Critical Defect Rate measures the percentage of defects classified

as Critical.



\### Formula



Critical Defect Rate (%) = Critical Defects / Total Defects × 100



\### Example Input



\- Total Defects = 8

\- Critical Defects = 2



\### Calculation



2 / 8 × 100 = 25%



\### Expected Output



\*\*Critical Defect Rate = 25%\*\*



\---



\## 7. KPI 5 — Shipment Pass Rate



\### Definition



Shipment Pass Rate measures the percentage of inspected units that

are cleared for shipment.



\### Formula



Shipment Pass Rate (%) = Units Passed / Units Inspected × 100



\### Example Input



\- Units Inspected = 100

\- Units Passed for Shipment = 94



\### Calculation



94 / 100 × 100 = 94%



\### Expected Output



\*\*Shipment Pass Rate = 94%\*\*



\---



\## 8. KPI 6 — First-Time Rejection Rate

\### Formula



First-Time Rejection Rate (%) =

First-Time Rejections / Total Units Inspected × 100



\### Example Input



\- Total Units Inspected = 100

\- First-Time Rejections = 8



\### Calculation



8 / 100 × 100 = 8%

\*\*First-Time Rejection Rate = 8%\*\*

\## 10. Management KPI Review



QC management should monitor these KPIs regularly to identify:



\- Quality deterioration

\- Recurring defects

\- Increased rework

\- Critical defect trends

\- Supplier quality problems

\- Hardware quality issues

\- Software quality issues

\- Shipment readiness problems



\---



\## 11. Recommended Management Actions



If FPY decreases:



1\. Identify the highest-frequency defects.

2\. Perform Root Cause Analysis (RCA).

3\. Identify HW, SW, EDGE or supplier ownership.

4\. Track corrective actions.

5\. Monitor rework trends.

6\. Review supplier performance.

7\. Measure KPI improvement after corrective action.
