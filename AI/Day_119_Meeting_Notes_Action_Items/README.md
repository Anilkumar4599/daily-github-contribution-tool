\# Day 119 — Meeting Notes to Action Items



\## Project Overview



This project creates a reusable AI prompt that converts raw meeting notes into structured action items, decisions, pending clarifications, and follow-up requirements.



\## Business Use Cases



\- Procurement review meetings

\- Supplier follow-up meetings

\- QC and defect review meetings

\- Service escalation meetings

\- Weekly management review meetings



\## Project Files



| File | Purpose |

|---|---|

| Meeting\_Notes\_Action\_Items\_Prompt.md | Complete reusable prompt, sample input, expected output, and test cases |



\## Key Features



\- Extracts clear action items from unstructured notes.

\- Identifies owners and target dates when supplied.

\- Separates decisions from discussion points.

\- Highlights missing owners and deadlines.

\- Classifies priority and action status.

\- Identifies risks, blockers, and pending clarifications.

\- Avoids inventing missing information.



\## Validation



Three test cases are included to verify:



1\. Missing owner handling.

2\. Missing deadline handling.

3\. Correct separation of discussion points and formal decisions.



\## Learning Outcome



Learned how to convert informal business meeting notes into a structured, actionable management tracker using AI prompt engineering.
