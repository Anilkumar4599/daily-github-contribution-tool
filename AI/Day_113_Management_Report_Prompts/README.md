\# Day 113 — AI Management-Report Generation Prompts



\## Project Overview



A practical library of 10 reusable AI prompts for management reporting across Procurement, Supplier Performance, Quality Control, Inventory, Service Operations, KPIs, and Cost Management.



\## Objectives



\- Standardise management reporting.

\- Convert operational data into actionable findings.

\- Identify business risks and overdue actions.

\- Support KPI and cost analysis.

\- Improve the clarity of management decisions.

\- Validate source data before generating reports.



\## Files



\- `Management\_Report\_Prompts.md` — Ten prompts, sample procurement data, expected calculations, and three test cases.

\- `README.md` — Project overview, usage, and validation checklist.



\## How to Use



1\. Select a prompt for the report you need.

2\. Supply the relevant source data and reporting period.

3\. Validate the input data.

4\. Review the generated calculations and findings.

5\. Check missing information and assumptions.

6\. Verify the report against the original records.

7\. Share the report only after the appropriate review.



\## Test Cases



1\. Verify order totals and status counts.

2\. Verify overdue open orders and late deliveries.

3\. Verify missing-data detection and handling.



\## Limitations



The sample data is fictional. AI-generated conclusions and proposed actions require human review before management use.
