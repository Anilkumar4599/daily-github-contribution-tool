\# Day 115 — Excel Executive Summary Prompt



\## Project Overview



A reusable AI prompt for converting raw Excel or tabular business data into a concise, decision-focused executive summary.



\## Business Applications



\- Procurement and overdue purchase orders

\- Supplier performance

\- QC defect analysis

\- Inventory and stock-out risks

\- Service performance

\- Operational KPI reporting



\## Files



\- `Excel\_Executive\_Summary\_Prompt.md` — Reusable prompt, sample input, expected report, and three test cases.

\- `README.md` — Project overview and usage instructions.



\## How to Use



1\. Prepare the Excel file or copy the relevant table.

2\. State the reporting date and period.

3\. Explain the management question or business objective.

4\. Provide the data to the AI tool.

5\. Review the data-validation findings.

6\. Check important totals and calculations against the source.

7\. Review risks and recommended actions before sharing the report.



\## Validation



Three test cases cover:

1\. Correct totals and status counts.

2\. Correct identification of overdue open orders.

3\. Safe handling of missing due dates.



\## Limitations



The example data is fictional. AI-generated reports require validation against actual source records. Missing data must be identified rather than invented.
